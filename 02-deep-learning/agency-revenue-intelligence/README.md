# Agency Revenue Intelligence & Forecasting Platform

An end-to-end business forecasting platform for a digital marketing agency. It combines **agency financial revenue forecasting** with **client campaign revenue forecasting**, supporting scheduled forecasts and near-real-time monitoring.

> **Project status:** Architecture and repository scaffold initialized. No model has been trained and no performance results are claimed yet.

## Business objectives

### A. Agency financial forecasting
Forecast the agency's own recognized revenue using client contracts, retainers, project fees, renewals, new-business pipeline, churn, invoices and collections.

### B. Campaign revenue forecasting
Forecast the revenue generated by clients' advertising campaigns using spend, impressions, clicks, leads, conversions, channel, campaign attributes and historical attributed revenue.

These are separate targets: campaign revenue belongs to the agency's clients; agency revenue is the fee/retainer income earned by the agency. They must not be combined or double-counted.

## Planned capabilities

- Daily campaign and conversion monitoring.
- Weekly client and campaign risk signals.
- Monthly and quarterly agency revenue forecasts.
- LSTM/GRU and temporal deep-learning experiments, compared with statistical and tree-based baselines.
- Forecast intervals, forecast bias and error analysis.
- Scenario simulation for budget, renewal, acquisition and churn assumptions.
- Scheduled batch inference and event-driven inference.
- API, dashboard, persistence, tests, containerization and model monitoring.

## Architecture

```text
CRM / Contracts / Finance / Campaign performance
                    |
          Validation + ingestion
             /             \
  Scheduled batch       Event stream
       |                    |
       +---- Feature processing ----+
                    |
          Versioned forecasting models
       (baselines, LSTM/GRU, TCN, Transformer)
                    |
       Forecasts + intervals + risk signals
                    |
       PostgreSQL / FastAPI / Streamlit
                    |
       Monitoring, alerts and reports
```

The first real-time milestone will replay timestamped historical events through a local stream processor. This demonstrates event-driven processing without claiming access to a live agency data feed.

## Data strategy

Public campaign datasets will provide campaign-performance examples. Agency-specific contracts, retainers, invoices, renewals and payment records will be generated as **synthetic data** with documented assumptions. Synthetic records will be clearly labelled and will never be presented as real company financial data.

Raw, interim and processed data are kept separate. Do not commit private client data, credentials, large datasets or trained model binaries.

## Evaluation

Use chronological train/validation/test splits and compare against simple baselines. Report MAE, RMSE, WAPE and forecast bias; evaluate prediction-interval coverage when intervals are produced. Avoid random splitting of time-series observations and prevent future information from leaking into features.

## Responsible interpretation

Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees. Scenario outputs depend on explicit assumptions and historical associations; they do not establish causal effects of changing ad budgets. Financial metrics must distinguish recognized revenue, invoiced amounts, cash collected and client-attributed campaign sales.
