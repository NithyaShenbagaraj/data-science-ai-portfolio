# Notebooks

Notebooks are for exploration and documented experiments. Reusable production logic belongs in `src/`.

Planned sequence:
1. Business data understanding and source audit.
2. Campaign EDA and SQL analytics.
3. Agency financial data generation and validation.
4. Agency revenue feature engineering.
5. Campaign feature engineering.
6. Statistical and machine-learning baselines.
7. LSTM and GRU experiments.
8. Advanced temporal models and evaluation.

Use chronological splits, fixed random seeds where applicable, and record dataset versions. Do not report results until the notebook has been executed against the documented data.
