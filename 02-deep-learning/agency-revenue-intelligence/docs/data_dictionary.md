# Data Dictionary and Metric Definitions

All datasets generated by this project are synthetic. They are intended for reproducible learning and must not be presented as actual company or client financial records.

## Agency finance

| Field | Grain | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| month | Month | Month-end timestamp |
| recognized_agency_revenue | Agency-month | Retainer revenue plus project-fee revenue earned in the month |
| retainer_revenue_inr | Agency-month | Monthly recurring service fees recognized |
| project_fee_revenue_inr | Agency-month | Non-recurring project fees recognized |
| active_clients | Agency-month | Distinct clients active during the month |
| new_clients | Agency-month | Clients acquired during the month |

## Campaign performance

| Field | Grain | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| date | Campaign-day | Calendar date |
| campaign_id | Campaign-day | Campaign identifier |
| channel | Campaign-day | Marketing channel |
| spend_inr | Campaign-day | Advertising spend |
| impressions | Campaign-day | Ad impressions |
| clicks | Campaign-day | Clicks |
| leads | Campaign-day | Leads attributed to clicks |
| conversions | Campaign-day | Customers attributed to leads |
| attributed_campaign_revenue | Campaign-day | Client sales attributed to campaign conversions |

## Derived metrics

- Click-through rate = clicks / impressions.
- Click-to-lead rate = leads / clicks.
- Lead-to-customer rate = conversions / leads.
- ROAS = attributed campaign revenue / advertising spend.
- ROAS is not gross margin, contribution margin, or profit.
- Agency recognized revenue, invoice value, cash collections, and campaign-attributed sales are distinct measures and must not be combined.

## Modeling safeguards

- Use chronological train/validation/test splits.
- Build lag and rolling features from prior observations only.
- Split campaign data by unique calendar date, not individual campaign-day rows.
- Fit scalers and models using training history only.
- Report MAE, RMSE, WAPE, and signed forecast bias.
- Do not interpret historical campaign ROAS as a causal estimate of incremental budget return.
